# Slide 3 — What is Terra? (Vision & Introduction)

## Slide Title
Meet Terra — Your Carbon Diary, Strategist & Companion

## The One-Liner
Terra is a **domain-agnostic carbon accounting intelligence platform** that tracks every activity event → emission factor → tCO₂e, and turns that data into actionable, personalised reduction recommendations.

## What Terra Is
| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Atomic Tracker** | Logs every litre, kWh, km, kg, and hour as individual "activity events" |
| **Personal Diary** | Your day-to-day carbon journal — what you did, what it cost the planet, what you could do better |
| **Production Companion** | For film/TV sets, construction sites, logistics fleets — any domain |
| **Intelligence Layer** | ML-powered forecasting, anomaly detection, and confidence scoring |
| **Suggestion Engine** | Specific, costed recommendations: "Switch 30% diesel to HVO → save X tCO₂e for £Y" |

## First Vertical: Film & TV Production
- Predict emissions at **greenlight** (before shooting begins)
- Track emissions **live** during production
- Generate **audit-ready** BAFTA albert / PEAR reports
- Flag anomalies automatically

## Visual Direction

- Four small icon tiles below: Diary (notebook), Companion (handshake), Brain (lightbulb), Scale (growing plant)

## Speaker Notes
Terra is three things at once. One: a diary that never forgets a carbon decision you made today. Two: a companion that sits beside you on set or in the office and whispers "there's a cheaper, cleaner way to do this." Three: a strategist that learns from every project you've ever done and gets smarter every season.
