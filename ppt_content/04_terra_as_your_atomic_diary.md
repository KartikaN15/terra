# Slide 4 — Terra as Your Atomic Diary

## Slide Title
Your Day, Your Decisions, Your Data — Atom by Atom

## The Diary Concept
Terra treats **every carbon-relevant action** as a diary entry. Not summaries. Not estimates. **Atoms.**

## A Day in the Life (Film Production Example)

| Time | What Happened | Terra Logs | kgCO₂e |
|------|--------------|------------|--------|
| 06:00 | Diesel generator refuelled (45L) | Scope 1 · Energy · diesel_generator | 120.6 |
| 08:30 | Crew shuttle from hotel (12 km × 15 people) | Scope 3 · Transport · medium_car_diesel | 30.8 |
| 10:00 | Studio grid electricity (85 kWh) | Scope 2 · Energy · grid_electricity_uk | 17.6 |
| 13:00 | Lunch served (beef for 40) | Scope 3 · Catering · beef_average | 2,400.0 |
| 17:00 | Waste collected (mixed, 12 kg) | Scope 3 · Waste · landfill_general | 6.0 |
| 18:00 | Producer flies back to London (short-haul, 340 km) | Scope 3 · Transport · short_haul_flight × 1.7 RF | 90.1 |

**Daily Total: ~2,665 kgCO₂e** (≈ 2.67 tCO₂e)

## What the Diary Gives You
1. **Real-Time Awareness** — See your footprint as it happens, not in a quarterly report
2. **Pattern Recognition** — "Catering is 45% of our daily emissions. Did we notice that last week?"
3. **Accountability** — Every entry has a source, a person, a timestamp, a confidence score
4. **Emotional Connection** — You don't abstractly "care about climate." You see *your* flight, *your* generator, *your* lunch.

## For Any Domain
| Domain | Today's Diary Entry |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Film/TV** | Generator fuel, crew travel, VFX render hours |
| **Construction** | Concrete poured, steel delivered, site diesel, worker commute |
| **Logistics** | Route km, refrigerated truck hours, pallet packaging |
| **Corporate** | Office electricity, business travel, cloud compute, catering |

## Speaker Notes
This is the atomic promise. Terra doesn't ask you to care about the whole world's emissions. It asks you to log what happened today. One generator refill. One shuttle run. One lunch order. When you see that beef catering for 40 people emitted 2,400 kg of CO₂ — more than everything else combined — you don't need a climate scientist to tell you what to change tomorrow.
