## The Numbers at Scale

### Atomic Level (You, Today)
- Switch one crew shuttle to an EV: **-4.2 kgCO₂e per day**
- Choose plant-based catering for 40 people: **-1,800 kgCO₂e per lunch**
- Use HVO instead of diesel for one generator: **-90% emissions per litre**

### Production Level (One Project)
- Typical medium film: **~769 tCO₂e**
- Terra-optimised medium film: **~520 tCO₂e**
- **Savings: 249 tCO₂e ≈ planting 4,150 trees**

### Studio Level (Annual Portfolio)
- 20 productions per year
- Unoptimised: **15,380 tCO₂e**
- Terra-optimised: **10,400 tCO₂e**
- **Savings: 4,980 tCO₂e ≈ taking 1,080 cars off the road for a year**

### Industry Level
- UK film/TV: **175,000 tCO₂e/year**
- Global streaming production: **~1.5M tCO₂e/year**
- If 30% of productions use Terra-level optimisation: **-450,000 tCO₂e/year**

## The Power of Atomic Habits
> "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems." — James Clear

Terra makes carbon-awareness a **system**, not a goal.
- The diary creates **habit loops** (log → see → adjust)
- The suggestions create **behavioural nudges** at the point of decision
- The confidence scores create **data integrity culture** (we only trust what we measure)
- The forecasts create **planning discipline** (carbon budget = financial budget)

## Speaker Notes
This is the vision slide. The atomic approach isn't small thinking — it's the only thinking that works at scale. No one decides to "save the planet" at 6 AM. But someone does decide to log 45 litres of diesel. And when Terra shows them that HVO would cut 90% of those emissions for a modest cost, that one decision ripples. Multiply by 200 decisions per week, per person, per production, per studio, per industry. That's how you move from atomic to planetary.
