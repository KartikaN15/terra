# Slide 8 — How Terra Suggests: The OED Playbook

## Slide Title
Don't Just Measure — Optimise, Electrify, Decarbonise

## The Philosophy
Terra never says *"use less energy."*  
Terra says: *"Switch 30% of your diesel generators to HVO fuel. You'll cut 89 tCO₂e and it costs £2,500."*

## The OED Framework
Every suggestion falls into one of three playbooks:

| Playbook | Strategy | Example |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **O** — Optimise | Do less, do smarter, reduce waste | Consolidate travel days, boost recycling, batch VFX renders |
| **E** — Electrify | Switch to low-carbon energy | Renewable grid tariffs, EV shuttles, green cloud regions |
| **D** — Decarbonise | Eliminate the emission source | Plant-based catering, modular reusable sets, green hotels |

## Live Recommendation Examples

### Scenario 1: High Energy Production
**Data:** Energy = 35% of total footprint, mostly diesel generators

| Action | Saving | Cost | Effort | Impact |
|--------|--------|------|--------|--------|
| Switch 30% diesel → HVO | -89 tCO₂e | £2,500 | Low | High |
| Purchase 100% renewable grid | -45 tCO₂e | £1,200 | Low | High |
| Add LED volume stage | -32 tCO₂e | £5,000 | Medium | Medium |

### Scenario 2: Travel-Heavy Production
**Data:** Transport = 40% of total footprint

| Action | Saving | Cost | Effort | Impact |
|--------|--------|------|--------|--------|
| Consolidate location travel | -28 tCO₂e | £0 | Low | Medium |
| Block-book green hotels | -15 tCO₂e | £0 | Low | Medium |
| Virtual production for B-roll | -18 tCO₂e | £3,000 | Medium | Medium |

### Scenario 3: Catering-Heavy Production
**Data:** Catering = 12% of total footprint (beef-dominated)

| Action | Saving | Cost | Effort | Impact |
|--------|--------|------|--------|--------|
| Plant-based default 2 days/week | -42 tCO₂e | **-£400** (saves money) | Low | High |

## Cost-Carbon Trade-off Engine
Terra explicitly surfaces when sustainability **saves money**:
- Plant-based catering: cheaper + lower carbon
- Reusable modular sets: rental vs. buy saves materials + disposal
- Virtual production: fewer location days = less travel + less accommodation

## Confidence-Weighted Prioritisation
Recommendations are ranked by:
1. **Estimated tCO₂e saving** (highest first)
2. **Effort vs. Impact** matrix (quick wins floated to top)
3. **Domain context** (VFX-heavy productions see render-farm suggestions; location-heavy see travel suggestions)

## Visual Direction
- Three vertical pillars labeled O · E · D with clay-render icons
- O: A compact carpool van (Optimise)
- E: A solar panel + lightning bolt (Electrify)
- D: A sprout growing from soil (Decarbonise)
- Bottom: A card showing one specific recommendation with saving, cost, and a green "Apply" button

## Speaker Notes
This is the slide that converts sceptics. Everyone has heard "go green." Nobody has heard "go green and save £400 on catering while cutting 42 tonnes of CO₂." Terra's suggestions are specific, costed, and prioritised. The OED framework gives structure to chaos. And because every recommendation is grounded in your actual production data — not generic best practices — it actually applies to you.
