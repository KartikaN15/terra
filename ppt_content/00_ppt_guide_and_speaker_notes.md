## Design System

### Palette
| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Primary | `#10B981` | Emerald — main brand, positive metrics, growth |
| Deep | `#065F46` | Forest — headers, emphasis |
| Accent | `#F59E0B` | Amber — alerts, over-budget, warnings |
| Neutral | Slate-50 → Slate-900 | Text, backgrounds, hierarchy |

### Typography
- **UI**: Inter
- **Display / Hero Numbers**: General Sans or Cabinet Grotesk

### Illustration Style
- **3D clay render** — matte plasticine finish, soft studio lighting
- Rounded geometry, pastel palette anchored in emerald + cream + amber
- Isometric or three-quarter view
- Faceless-friendly characters (dot eyes, no mouths)
- Reference: Bella Illustration / Oleg Beresnev style

### Chart Style
- Recharts with rounded bar caps
- Soft gradients, minimal gridlines
- Emerald/amber/duotone — no rainbow categorical palettes

## Tone Guide

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Be warm and optimistic | Be corporate or preachy |
| Use specific numbers | Use vague phrases like "reduce emissions" |
| Show cost savings alongside carbon savings | Frame sustainability as expensive sacrifice |
| Speak to individuals ("your day", "your project") | Speak only to abstract organisations |
| Acknowledge imperfection ("log what you can") | Demand perfection or all-or-nothing |
