# Slide 2 — The Sustainability Crisis: Why This Matters

## Slide Title
The Invisible Weight We All Carry

## Key Statistics
- **1.27M tCO₂e** — Netflix corporate footprint (2024) alone
- **175,000 tCO₂e** — UK film/TV industry annual emissions (BAFTA albert)
- **48%** of a tentpole film's emissions come from fuel & generators
- **Scope 3** (value chain) accounts for **~90%** of most corporate footprints
- The average person makes **200+ carbon-relevant decisions** every week without knowing it

## The Core Problem
1. **Invisible Emissions** — Most carbon output happens unseen (supply chains, electricity grids, travel)
2. **Delayed Feedback** — You only learn your footprint months after the damage is done
3. **No Atomic Action** — Advice is generic ("use less plastic") instead of specific to *your* day, *your* project, *your* domain
4. **Cost vs. Planet** — Sustainability is framed as expensive; the cost-saving opportunities are missed

## The Analogy
> "Trying to reduce your carbon footprint without data is like trying to save money without looking at your bank statement."

## Visual Direction
- A 3D clay-render of three stacked planets (Scope 1, 2, 3) connected by soft ribbons
- Bottom: fuel pump & truck | Middle: power line & lightning | Top: airplane & shopping bag
- Faint carbon particles floating upward to show "invisible" emissions

## Speaker Notes
Set the emotional and intellectual stage. Sustainability isn't just a boardroom topic — it's a daily invisible burden. Every flight booked, every generator refuelled, every meal served on set adds up. The problem isn't that people don't care. It's that they can't *see* it in real time. Terra changes that.
